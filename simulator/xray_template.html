Blood Choke X-Ray

What the model says happens inside the head when the carotid arteries are squeezed shut: blood stops reaching the front of the brain, its oxygen runs down in seconds, and it drops below the level the model treats as consciousness.

Educational model only. Not medical advice, and not for safety or training decisions. The drawing is a schematic, not a real scan, and it shows physiology, not technique.

What happened

    What is computed

    • The timing and the oxygen levels. They come from the same equation as the repo's Python model, run once for the front of the brain and once for the back.
    • How far the flow drops. It is the blood velocity measured in the brain's arteries during neck restraint (Mitchell 2012).
    • The threshold. It is fitted to 1943 data, not measured, and it is the weakest part of the model.

    What is illustration only

    • The anatomy. It is a hand-drawn schematic, not to scale. The left and right arteries overlap in this side view.
    • Which parts go dark. The brain is split into just two zones, a front fed by the carotids and a back fed by the vertebrals. Real brains share supply through connecting arteries, and the model cannot say which exact regions fail first.
    • The moving dots. Their speed shows more or less flow. It is not a real blood speed.
    • Waking up. The model recovers faster than people do, and that part has not been checked yet.
