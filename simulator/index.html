Brain Oxygen Simulator

A one-equation model of the oxygen held in the brain's blood. Cut the blood flow, watch the store run down, and see when it crosses the level the model treats as loss of consciousness.

Educational model only. This is not medical advice and must not be used for safety, training or medical decisions. It describes physiology, not technique, and its key threshold is fitted, not measured.

Scenario

Unconsciousness threshold

No paper we could open measures this. It is fitted to the 1943 data, and the fit allows anything inside the slider's range. This one setting moves the answer more than every other input.

Physiology

Sets how soon oxygen use slows as oxygen runs low. Sliders span the ranges used in the repo's uncertainty analysis.

What was actually measured

    The model

    dC/dt = F(t)·Ca − F(t)·C/V − M(C)

    C is the oxygen in the blood inside the brain. Blood brings oxygen in at rate F·Ca, carries what is left out at F·C/V, and the tissue uses M(C). The same equation lives in choke_model/model.py, solved there with SciPy; this page runs a copy of it that the repo's tests check against the Python version.

    • The brain is treated as one well-mixed box, so it cannot show the front of the brain failing while the back is still supplied.
    • In the repo's Monte Carlo run, fewer than half of the predictions for the 2012 study landed inside the observed range.
    • Recovery after release has not been checked against data yet.
